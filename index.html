      Live directions are still being developed

      Access BC's live, step-by-step directions are being tested. They aren't yet reliable enough to depend on as an assistive device.

      • Your location can be off by 15 to 30 feet or more, especially near buildings and indoors.
      • The map may be out of date: construction, closures, snow, and broken elevators or doors may not be shown.
      • Keep using your usual mobility skills, cane, or guide dog. Watch for hazards, and ask for help if you need it.

      Please proceed with caution, and tell us about problems through the feedback form.
